05Trust & privacy

How your school's data is handled

The application syncs data to a Google Sheet that the school owns and controls, and keeps an encrypted backup copy in the school's own Google Drive. Nothing is shared with, or visible to, any other school or third party. See the full Privacy Policy for details on the exact permissions requested.

Authentication & Access control

The school administrator authenticates with their school's Google account so the desktop application can sync data to that school's own Google Sheet and store a backup copy in that school's own Google Drive. Teacher access to the online portal is separately controlled through admin-issued PINs, not through Google sign-in.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Google Account Access

Mmodemmo School Management System requests only the Google permissions needed to sync and back up a school's own records:

  • Google Sheets — to read and write the school's own student, teacher, attendance, and fee records.
  • Google Drive (app-created files only) — to store an encrypted backup of the school's database in a folder the app creates inside that school's own Drive. The App cannot see or access the rest of a school's Drive.

Mmodemmo School Management System does not request access to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service, and does not use Google account data for advertising.

06FAQs
What happens if a school computer is lost or damaged?

Nothing is lost. Your live records are already synced to your school's own Google Sheet, and a full encrypted database backup is saved automatically to your school's own Google Drive. Install the app on a replacement computer, sign in with the same school Google account, and restore the latest backup to pick up exactly where you left off.

What if our school doesn't have reliable internet?

The desktop app is built offline-first — enter marks, attendance, and levies with no connection at all. It syncs to your school's Google Sheet and Drive backup automatically the next time you're online.

Does my school need internet to use the desktop app?

No — the desktop app works offline for day-to-day use. It syncs to your school's Google Sheet and Drive backup whenever you're connected.

Can Mmodemmo School Management App see files elsewhere in our Google Drive?

No. It only accesses the Sheet you connect and a folder it creates itself in your Drive for backups — nothing else in your account.

How do teachers get access without a Google sign-in?

The admin issues each teacher a PIN and a portal link scoped to their classes and duties. Access can be changed or revoked at any time.

Can more than one person use the app on the desktop at the same time?

The desktop application itself is used by one person at a time — it's installed on a single machine and only one account can be logged in at once, even though the admin can create multiple user accounts. Real concurrency comes from the online portal: teachers can record data at the same time as each other (and while the admin is using the desktop app) from their own phone or computer using their PIN, without conflicts, because the portal runs separately from the desktop application.

What happens if a teacher leaves the school or loses their PIN?

The admin can revoke or reissue any teacher's PIN at any time from the desktop app, immediately cutting off or restoring their access to the portal.
