Kofiekoye — Homework for the homework giver

A full term of Schemes of Learning and Lesson Plans, ready in minutes.

Kofiekoye is a Windows desktop application for Junior High School teachers in Ghana. Pick your subject, class and term, set your timetable once, and it builds your termly and annual Scheme of Learning and a one-page Lesson Plan for every period — aligned to the NaCCA Common Core Programme curriculum and Teacher's Resource Pack, and exported as editable Word documents and print-ready PDFs.

01What it does

Automatic Year Plan

Enter the number of weeks in each term and choose 2 or 3 lessons a week. Kofiekoye spreads every strand of the curriculum across the three terms in proportion, in curriculum order, so each term covers its fair share without you picking indicators week by week.

Scheme of Learning

Termly and annual Schemes of Learning with week-ending dates, strands, sub-strands, content standards, indicators and resources — laid out in the standard format your headteacher and SISO expect.

One-page Lesson Plans

A landscape, one-page lesson plan for every period: performance indicator, starter, main activities, assessment, resources, key words, plenary, homework, cross-curriculum links and learner difficulties.

Your timetable, your dates

Set the days, periods and times you teach once. Every generated lesson plan carries the right date, period, time and week-ending, week after week.

Word and PDF

Every document is saved as an editable Word file and a print-ready PDF, so you can adjust anything before you print or submit.

Built-in curriculum content

Activities, assessment questions, resources with no-projector alternatives, and homework written from the NaCCA curriculum exemplars for Basic 7, 8 and 9 — no internet or PDF hunting needed.

02How it works
1

Install and create your admin account

Run the installer, then create your administrator account. You receive a recovery key so you can reset your password yourself if you ever forget it.

2

Choose subject, class and academic year

Pick the subject and class level you teach (Basic 7, 8 or 9), enter the number of weeks in each term and your lessons per week, then click Compute Year Plan to see how the curriculum is spread.

3

Set your weekly timetable once

Tell it which days, periods and times your lessons fall on. It reuses them for every week.

4

Generate, edit, print

Pick the term and the first week-ending date, then generate the Scheme of Learning, the Annual Scheme of Learning and the Lesson Plans. Open the Word files to make any changes, or print the PDFs.

5

Prefer to plan week by week?

Use the Scheme of Learning (Week by Week) and Lesson Plan (Week by Week) tabs to choose indicators yourself and create a plan for one week or all weeks.

07FAQs
Which classes and curriculum does it follow?

Basic 7, 8 and 9 (JHS), using the NaCCA Common Core Programme (CCP) curriculum's strands, sub-strands, content standards, indicators and exemplars.

Do I need internet to use it?

No. Everyday use works fully offline; the curriculum content is built into the app.

Can I edit the lesson plans?

Yes. Every plan is also saved as a Word document, so you can change anything before printing.

Do I need Microsoft Word or any other software?

No extra software is needed to generate the PDFs. You only need Word (or another word processor) if you want to edit the Word files.

What if my term has more lessons than weeks?

The Automatic Year Plan fits the curriculum into the weeks and lessons per week you enter. If a term's share does not fit, it tells you what was left over instead of stopping.

I forgot my password. What now?

Use the recovery key you received when you created your admin account to reset it inside the app.

It says Windows blocked it from saving. What do I do?

Windows Security's Controlled folder access protects Documents, Desktop and Pictures. Save to another folder (for example a new folder on drive D:), or allow the app: Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → Manage ransomware protection → Allow an app through Controlled folder access → Add an allowed app → Kofiekoye.exe.

What computer do I need?

A Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit) laptop or desktop.

How much does it cost?

You pay once, per subject, with no expiry or yearly renewal. For the price, contact the developer on WhatsApp (0205334596).

Can I add a subject later, or move to a new computer?

Contact the developer on WhatsApp (0205334596) with the activation code from the computer you want to use.
