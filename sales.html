04FAQs

Questions schools ask before buying.

Tiers

What's the difference between the two tiers?

Desktop Only gives you the full desktop application — attendance, levies, SBA & mock exam marks, report cards, and BECE analysis, with automatic Google Drive backup. Desktop + Online Portal adds the online admin/teacher portal on top, so teachers can record attendance, levies, and marks from their own phone or computer using an admin-issued PIN.

Can I add the online portal later if I start with Desktop Only?

Yes — you can upgrade from Desktop Only to Desktop + Online Portal at any time. Your existing records carry over; nothing needs to be re-entered.

Setup & Access

Do I need internet to use the app?

The desktop app works fully offline. Only the online portal (for admin & teacher access), SMS sending, and backup sync need an internet connection.

Do teachers need to install anything?

No — on the Desktop + Online Portal tier, teachers use the online portal from any phone or computer browser with the PIN and link you give them; no software install required. On Desktop Only, there's no portal — teachers record on an Excel template or manually type records instead.

What if a teacher isn't comfortable using the portal?

On the Desktop + Online Portal tier, yes — you can hand out an Excel template instead, and they fill in marks manually for you to import. On Desktop Only, Excel is how all teachers record data, since there's no portal on that tier.

How many teachers or students can I add?

No limit — add as many teachers and students as your school needs.

Cost

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase with no recurring fees and no expiry date.

Are there any extra costs, like for SMS?

Yes — SMS results and notices are sent through a third-party SMS gateway (Arkesel), which bills separately at a low per-message rate. In practice this typically works out to around GH₵20–30 per term for a school, depending on how many messages are sent.

Do I get free updates?

Regular bug-fix updates are free. Major version upgrades — ones that add significant new functionality — may carry a separate upgrade fee.

How will I know when an update is available?

The app checks automatically each time it opens and shows a small in-app notice if a newer version exists — just click through to download it and run the new installer over the old one. No uninstalling, and your school data is untouched either way.

Data Safety

What happens if the computer is lost, stolen, or crashes?

Your data is backed up automatically to the school's own Google account. Reinstall the app on any new machine and restore everything from that backup.

Is our data shared with anyone, or with other schools?

No. All data stays inside your school's own Google account at all times — it is never shared with or visible to any other school or third party.

Who can see our student records?

Only staff the admin adds and grants access to. The admin controls every permission and can revoke access at any time.

Day-to-Day Use

How are report cards generated?

Automatically, as soon as marks are entered — both terminal and mock exam report cards, with no manual compilation.

Can I customize subjects and grading?

Yes — admins can rename or add subjects and adjust grading scales to match their school's own standard.

Can parents receive results directly?

Yes, by SMS — performance and mock exam results can be sent straight to parents, along with custom meeting or notice messages.

Can I get my data out if I ever stop using the app?

Yes — every record, including attendance, levies, and marks, can be exported to PDF or Excel at any time.

Support

What if something breaks or I don't understand a feature?

First, check the video tutorials on YouTube — most questions are answered there. You can also ask in our Telegram group, where common questions get answered for everyone. If you're still stuck or something's actually broken, contact the developer directly on WhatsApp or Telegram.

Is training provided when I buy?

You get access to step-by-step video tutorials on YouTube covering setup and every core feature. You'll also be added to our Telegram group, where you can ask questions and see answers to things other schools have run into. Still stuck? Reach out to the developer directly on WhatsApp or Telegram.
